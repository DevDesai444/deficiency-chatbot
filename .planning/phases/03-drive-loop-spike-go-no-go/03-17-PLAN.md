Folding them into "single-agent-scope-unreachable" would let the diagnosis blame the loop for a regex floor and would point Phase 4 at items it can never win.

Purpose: the whole integrity claim of this phase is that the criteria predate the results. This plan is where that becomes checkable.
Output: the reachability classification and the committed pre-registration, with a reviewer sign-off before the commit because it cannot be amended afterwards.
