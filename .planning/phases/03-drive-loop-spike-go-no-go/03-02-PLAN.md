<objective>
Make the grounding contract carry the information the Phase-3 gate has to read. Three additive schema changes, each of which RESEARCH.md located as a live gap:

1. **D-TEL3** — `emit_finding` has seven rejection sites and the reader cannot tell which half of the dual citation failed. `half=submission` + `not_byte_exact` means the model invented a quote; `half=rule` + `not_retrieved_this_session` means it never called `read_guideline`. Opposite diagnoses, opposite remedies, and today they land in the same bucket.
2. **D-TEL2** — `reason_code` is deliberately an open `str` so later plans add codes without editing `errors.py`. Telemetry needs a registry it can join against without copying the codes (a copy would drift and mislabel the single most diagnostic signal available).
3. **GROUND-03 / DETECT-04** — `emit_finding` validates `rule_span_id` thoroughly and then discards it (`emit_finding.py:112-117` keeps only `rule_citation or rule_span_id.doc_id` in `guidance_refs`), and stringifies `verdict` into `detail` at `:98`. The module documents both as accepted Phase-2 boundaries at `:94-111` because `schemas/faults.py` was off-limits. **Phase 3 is the later phase that entry names.**

Purpose: without these, the gate's most diagnostic signal is unreadable, the verdict is unscoreable, and a Phase-5 verifier cannot re-open the rule a finding cites.
Output: additive-only schema changes that leave every existing construction site and every committed golden fixture validating.
</objective>
