| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-05W4-02 | Information Disclosure | phase5-gate prints candidate counts to stdout | accept | Candidate counts are aggregate statistics (no verbatim submission content). Operator visibility is desired. |
| T-05W4-03 | Denial of Service | phase5-gate chains four gates sequentially; one gate's ingest blocks all | accept | All gates operate on the committed synthetic fixture (<50 KB); total latency is <120 seconds. The phase5-gate is a developer/CI tool, not a production endpoint. |
